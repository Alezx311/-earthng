EarthNG offline base map: Natural Earth 1:10m land, lakes, rivers, urban areas,
borders, populated places and country names; 1:110m land as a fallback. Public domain.
Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/tree/master/geojson
Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Country source data: OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0.
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
Country packs must retain their source/date and attribution in the manifest.

Shared art: EarthNG procedural drawings and CC0 photos (Panelka, ambientCG).
Retro City: original EarthNG procedural pixel drawings, palette and material preset.
No BeamNG game assets are included: game shapes and materials are referenced
from the player's installation. See the EarthNG source project for the art tools.
